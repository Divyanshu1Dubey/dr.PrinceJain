About Me

Researcher | Educator | Innovator

I am an Assistant Professor (Research Cadre) in the Department of Mechatronics Engineering, Parul Institute of Technology, Parul University, Vadodara, India. Recognized among the Top 2% Scientists Worldwide (Elsevier–Stanford, 2025), my work bridges Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Metamaterials, and Biomedical Signal Processing.

I hold a Ph.D. in Electronics Engineering from Punjab Engineering College (Deemed to be University), Chandigarh (CGPA 9/10), and have previously served at Chandigarh University and PEC as a faculty member. My research spans metamaterial absorbers/antennas (RF–THz–optical), polymer nanocomposites, wearable/IoT-based healthcare, and AI-driven materials design.
