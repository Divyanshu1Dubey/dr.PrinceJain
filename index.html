About Me

Researcher | Educator | Innovator

I am an Assistant Professor (Research Cadre) at the Department of Mechatronics Engineering, Parul Institute of Technology, Parul University, Vadodara, Gujarat, India. Recognized among the Top 2% Scientists Worldwide (Elsevier–Stanford, 2025), I am passionate about pushing the boundaries of research in Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, and Nanotechnology.

I hold a Ph.D. from Punjab Engineering College (Deemed to be University), Chandigarh, with a CGPA of 9/10. My research focuses on Metamaterial Absorbers/Antennas at RF, THz, and Visible Frequencies, Biomedical Signal Processing, and IoT applications. I have published over 100+ research papers in prestigious SCI/Scopus indexed journals and hold multiple design patents.
